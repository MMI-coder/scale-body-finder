# Image credits

Product images in `images/` are manufacturer promotional material, used for
identification and comparison. All rights remain with their respective owners:
**TBLeague (Phicen)**, **VeryCool**, and **Novan Studio**.

**This credit must also surface in the app UI, not only here.**

## Handling

These images are used **exactly as supplied** — no cropping, resizing, re-encoding
or other editing. Copies in this directory are byte-for-byte identical to the
source catalogue (verified by SHA-256). Anything that alters them is a bug.

Each row of `data/bodies.csv` names its own image in the `Image` column, and the
mapping is 1:1 — 23 rows, 23 files, no orphans either way.

The `S## Family.jpg` images each cover a whole TBLeague model line rather than a
single product code; the line members are listed in that row's `Notes`.
